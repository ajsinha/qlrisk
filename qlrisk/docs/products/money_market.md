# Money Market Products v1.1.0

## Overview

Short-term debt instruments with high liquidity and low risk.

## Products

| Product | Maturity | Features |
|---------|----------|----------|
| **Certificate of Deposit (CD)** | 1 week - 5 years | Fixed rate, FDIC insured |
| **Banker's Acceptance** | 30-180 days | Trade finance, bank guaranteed |
| **Eurodollar Deposit** | Overnight - 1 year | USD deposits outside US |
| **Commercial Paper** | 1-270 days | Unsecured corporate debt |
| **Federal Funds** | Overnight | Interbank lending |
| **Money Market Fund** | Open-ended | Diversified MM portfolio |

## Pricing

Money market instruments are priced on a discount basis:
```
Price = Face Value / (1 + r × days/360)
```

## Risk Characteristics

- Very short duration
- Minimal interest rate risk
- Credit risk depends on issuer
- High liquidity
