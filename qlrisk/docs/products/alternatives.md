# Alternative Products v1.1.0

## Overview

Alternative investments including digital assets, real estate, carbon credits, and private markets.

## Digital Assets (Cryptocurrency)

| Product | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Bitcoin (BTC)** | Spot cryptocurrency position |
| **Ethereum (ETH)** | Spot cryptocurrency position |
| **Crypto Future** | Exchange-traded crypto futures |
| **Crypto Perpetual** | No-expiry futures with funding rate |
| **Crypto Option** | Options on cryptocurrency |

### Risk Characteristics
- **Volatility:** 60-100% annualized
- **Correlation:** Low correlation to traditional assets
- **Custody Risk:** Counterparty risk with exchanges
